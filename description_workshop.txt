Removes the hunger mechanic from Get Fixed Boi and The Constant worlds.

I made this mod because I wanted to do a run of the GetFixedBoi seed but I found the hunger mechanic to just be annoying and not add much to the game.

However, you can also use this mod in the Constant seed if you want to for some reason.

Github: https://github.com/LongRamen/NoHunger
